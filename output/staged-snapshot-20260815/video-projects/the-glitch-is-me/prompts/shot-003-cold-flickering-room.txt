SHOT shot-003 — Cold Flickering Room

NARRATIVE INTENT
Show physical isolation inside an unstable digital frame.

SHOT PROMPT
A sparse cold room made from wet concrete and smoked glass flickers between solid reality and a precise wireframe absence. The Quantum Siren sits alone at the far edge of a narrow bed, almost motionless, while the light above her fails in slow irregular pulses. The camera tracks laterally behind translucent foreground glass, producing deep parallax and emotional distance.
