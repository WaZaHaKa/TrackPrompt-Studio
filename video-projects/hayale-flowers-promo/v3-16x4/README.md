# Hayale V3: 16 x 4

`scenario.json` is the versioned creative and timing source. Sixteen cuts at
24 fps produce 96 frames each and 1,536 frames overall. The Pydantic contract
also enforces role placement, three customer scenes and supported profile limits.
`schemas/hayale-edit.schema.json` describes its portable structural shape.

Use the repository root [operator runbook](../../../docs/hayale-16x4-runbook.md).
Preparation registers a plan in the existing Mission Control store; this folder
does not hold private selections, authorization, credentials or generated media.
The historical V2.1 run and delivery retain their own unchanged interpretation.

The default still animatic is entirely local. Motion preview, draft and final
are separate explicit profiles. Each profile binds selected asset hashes, source
duration/in-point, edited timing, model settings and budget. No automatic final
upgrade, scene reroll or RTX fallback exists.

Missing customer photos, approved logos and shop/product selections are required
inputs, not placeholder people or invented business material. The soundtrack
requires an approved explicit 64-second arrangement of the unchanged original.
