# Hayale GCP-only source selection

`source-map.json` binds one original logo and fourteen distinct original JPEGs:
three actual customer photos, two exterior views, three product arrangements,
three atmosphere details and three interior-tour views. It uses the clarified
sixteen-scene ordering, with the same logo source at both ends. All sixteen
video scenes must be generated in GCP; this map does not authorize reuse of the
historical 11-generated/5-local film as the new master.

Selection was made by inspecting all six existing Hayale contact sheets and
larger private discovery previews of the chosen originals. Every selected image
fully decoded. These previews are inspection evidence only and must never be
uploaded as the original or used as production seeds. Only the exact original
files named by relative path and SHA-256 are production inputs.

The three interior views are different actual photographs of the shop: a wide
room overview, the lower bouquet-display layout, and an oblique view of the glass
display shelves. The third view replaces a rejected candidate dominated by a
CCTV monitor. Its explicit composition crop is a cloud seed-preparation recipe,
not a locally processed asset. Customer and product recipes retain the complete
photographic subject, without geometric stretching. Logo preparation preserves
the original artwork and lettering.

The soundtrack hash identifies the approved original used by the historical
Hayale provenance. Its explicit 64-second arrangement is reused as an edit
recipe and must be rendered in GCP from the original source. No locally arranged
audio or historical completed film is a substitute source.

The map contains no absolute private filesystem paths. Resolve its relative
paths against the launcher's explicitly configured source root, check every
hash, and fail on missing or changed originals. Do not rediscover arbitrary
replacement images automatically. Additional photography remains outside this
selected upload set. Descriptions document visible content; final generated
likeness, lettering and motion still require output review.
