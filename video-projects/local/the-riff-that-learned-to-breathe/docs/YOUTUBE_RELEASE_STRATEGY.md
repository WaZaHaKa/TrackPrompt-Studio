# YouTube release strategy

The previous videos were expensive but did not earn many views, so this project must improve the audience-facing package as well as production cost.

## Working title

**DJ WaZaHaKa × Nir Pache — The Riff That Learned to Breathe | Anime Music Video**

Use the project title as a working title until the final track name is confirmed.

## First 15 seconds

Do not begin with a logo, long fade, or credit card. Show the Architect finishing the metal bird, its amber heart activating, and the launch into the conduit. By 00:15 the viewer should already understand that an idea is traveling from one creator to another.

## Thumbnail concept

A close composition of two adult fictional hands from opposite sides holding the same hybrid origami bird. The bird's amber heart is the focal point; one wing is metal and the other paper/fabric. Iron-blue background, restrained warm rim light, no small text. Export three candidates from shots 011, 014, and 015 and score them at small mobile size.

## Description hook

Open the description with the concrete story: a progressive-metal MIDI riff was slowed, rearranged, and rebuilt as lofi downtempo while its original musical heart was kept. Credit Nir Pache and DJ WaZaHaKa immediately.

## Retention choices

- A visible narrative question is established early: what will happen to the mechanical bird?
- Every four scenes form a clear act with a new visual promise.
- The solo motif becomes the first large external flight at shot 008.
- Shot 014 is the strongest movement/climax.
- Shot 016 resolves the connection cleanly instead of ending as an unrelated visual loop.

## Series identity

This can become the first entry in a repeatable WZHK Media series of original local anime music videos. Keep the same typography and color system in the YouTube thumbnail and channel art, but do not bake text into the video-generation frames.

## Measurement

After release, record impressions, click-through rate, first-30-second retention, average percentage viewed, traffic source, and subscriber conversion. Compare the opening and thumbnail against the prior realistic Veo videos rather than judging only by total views.
