# Character and prop reference prompts

Generate reference sheets before the 16 keyframes. These are fictional adult characters and must not resemble DJ WaZaHaKa or Nir Pache.

## The Architect

Prompt:

`The Architect, one consistent fictional adult with wavy dark hair, warm brown eyes, a rust-colored work jacket over a charcoal shirt, practical dark trousers, calm precise hands, no jewelry, no logos, neutral expression, front view, three-quarter view, profile view, full body and head-and-shoulders panels, consistent proportions and wardrobe, clean pale paper background, original hand-drawn anime character design, fine ink lines, restrained watercolor shading, iron-blue shadows and warm rust accents, no text, no labels, no logo`

Required anchors:

- Clearly adult.
- Wavy dark hair.
- Warm brown eyes.
- Rust work jacket over charcoal shirt.
- Same face, body proportions, and clothing in every view.

## The Listener

Prompt:

`The Listener, one consistent fictional adult with short dark hair and one silver forelock, an indigo overshirt, a muted amber scarf, charcoal trousers, observant eyes, no jewelry, no logos, neutral expression, front view, three-quarter view, profile view, full body and head-and-shoulders panels, consistent proportions and wardrobe, clean pale paper background, original hand-drawn anime character design, fine ink lines, restrained watercolor shading, indigo shadows and muted amber accent, no text, no labels, no logo`

Required anchors:

- Clearly adult.
- Short dark hair with exactly one silver forelock.
- Indigo overshirt.
- Muted amber scarf.
- Same face, body proportions, and clothing in every view.

## The Riff Bird

Prompt:

`the same palm-sized origami bird, initially made from brushed dark metal and guitar-string-like ribs, with a small warm amber mechanical heart visible through its chest; later its feathers become layered paper and fabric while the amber heart and exact silhouette remain unchanged, orthographic front, side, top, folded-wing and open-wing views, exact consistent silhouette, detailed amber heart placement, brushed dark metal version and transformed indigo paper-and-fabric version shown side by side, original hand-drawn anime prop design, fine ink lines, watercolor material callouts without words, pale paper background, no text, no logo`

Required anchors:

- Palm-sized scale.
- Exactly one amber mechanical heart.
- Same beak, eye placement, wing span, and tail silhouette.
- Hybrid form keeps the original metal ribs visible beneath paper and fabric.

## Reference selection policy

Generate at most four candidates per sheet, score them automatically for prompt compliance and visual consistency, and choose one deterministic winner. Store the approved sheets in the project archive. Do not stop the workflow for a repeated copyright approval; the collaboration rights record is already satisfied. A non-blocking contact sheet may still be produced for human review.
