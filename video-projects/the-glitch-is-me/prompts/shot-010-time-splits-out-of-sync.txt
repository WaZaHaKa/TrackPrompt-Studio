SHOT shot-010 — Time Splits Out of Sync

NARRATIVE INTENT
Represent the bridge's temporal fracture without a conventional glitch montage.

SHOT PROMPT
The Quantum Siren walks through a narrow dark corridor while three translucent temporal echoes of her body separate by a few inches, each moving at a slightly different phase. The corridor itself remains stable, but droplets, fabric and her shadow fall out of synchronization. One slow forward tracking shot; the layers nearly reunite at the end and then miss.
